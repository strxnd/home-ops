# GLITG SMP Rules

Every rule, ban, and modification exists to keep the server fair and enjoyable.
Deliberately bypassing the intention of a rule may still result in punishment
even if the exact method is not explicitly listed.

## Hearts And Death Bans

- When a player dies, they lose 1 heart and their killer gains 1 heart.
- The maximum is 20 hearts.
- During Week 1, the minimum is 3 hearts.
- During Week 2, the minimum is 1 heart.
- If a player is killed while already at the current minimum number of hearts, no heart is lost or gained.
- Players can only be death-banned on the final day.
- Players who cannot play on the final day but want to be death-banned may contact the admins.
- There are no revives.
- Withdrawn hearts count as crafted hearts.
- Crafted hearts cannot be equipped while a player is at or above 10 hearts.

## Legendary Items

- Hearts, the Mace, and the unique Netherite Chestplate are legendary items.
- Legendary items cannot be stored in chests, Ender chests, chest boats, barrels, bundles, item frames, furnaces, shelves, or hoppers.
- If a legendary item disappears or is accidentally destroyed, admins may recreate it.
- The Netherite Chestplate is unbreakable.

## Server Start And World

- There is a 1-hour server-wide grace period when the SMP begins.
- The grace period begins globally and does not start separately when each player first joins.
- The Nether is open from the start.
- The world border is 4,000 x 4,000 blocks.
- Keep farms, redstone machines, and other automated systems to a reasonable size.
- Farms or machines causing excessive lag may be reduced or removed by the admins.

## Bases And Raiding

- Public bases are mandatory.
- Every base must have a sign showing whose base it is.
- Every base must have a visible section above ground.
- Raiding someone's base while they are offline is not allowed.
- Destroying someone's base without a valid reason is not allowed.
- Killing an unarmoured or naked player without a valid reason is not allowed.
- Valid reasons include picking up or stealing loot, attacking you, or intentionally interfering with or provoking you.

## Post-Death Protection

- Players receive 30 minutes of PvP invulnerability after dying.
- Attacking another player immediately removes the protection.
- Post-death protection cannot be abused to interfere with fights, protect loot, block players, or gain an unfair advantage.
- Abuse of post-death protection will be handled by the admins.

## Teams

- A team may have no more than 2 high-tier players.
- High-tier classifications are decided by the admins.
- A high-tier player dying once to a mid-tier or low-tier player does not automatically change their classification.

## Armour, Weapons, And Enchantments

- Armour and weapons are limited to Diamond.
- Netherite may only be applied to pickaxes, shovels, and hoes.
- Netherite combat weapons and normal Netherite armour are banned.
- The server's unique Netherite Chestplate is the only exception to the Netherite armour ban.
- Protection is limited to Protection III.
- Sharpness is limited to Sharpness III.
- Bows are limited to Power IV.
- Density and Breach are limited to III.
- Wind Burst is limited to I.
- Thorns, Fire Aspect, Punch, and Lunge are banned.

## The Mace

- A maximum of 2 Maces may exist on the server.
- The first player to craft a Mace receives Mace #1, the only Mace that may
  ever be crafted normally.
- Mace #2 is awarded manually to the legitimate Dragon Egg holder.
- There are no additional Mace-specific enchantment restrictions beyond the server's normal enchantment rules.
- The Mace can deal a maximum of 8 hearts of damage in a single hit.

## Dragon Egg And Netherite Chestplate

- The Dragon Egg behaves normally and is obtained through normal gameplay.
- The server's only Netherite Chestplate is crafted from 1 Dragon Egg and 8 Diamonds.
- The Netherite Chestplate is unbreakable and is a legendary item.

## Potions, Totems, And Pearls

- Strength II potions automatically have an 8-minute duration.
- Speed I is allowed. Speed II is banned.
- Poison, Turtle Master, Slow Falling, Weakness, Slowness, and all tipped arrows are banned.
- Totems of Undying are completely banned and may not be carried, stored, or used.
- Ender Pearls are banned.

## Combat Restrictions

The following are banned during combat:

- Elytra.
- Riptide Tridents. Non-Riptide Tridents are allowed.
- Lava buckets, ice, Fire Aspect, Lunge, and Punch.
- Bucket or sponge draining.
- Combat logging and danger logging.
- Restocking or switching to backup armour sets.

## Damage And Kit Limits

- TNT minecarts can deal a maximum of 10 hearts of damage.
- PvP kits are limited to Protection III Diamond armour, Sharpness III Diamond weapons, and Power IV bows.
- Maximum 6 healing potions.
- Maximum 3 stacks of XP bottles.
- Maximum 2 stacks of cobwebs.
- Maximum 2 stacks of golden apples.
- Maximum 1 stack of Breeze Rods.
- Spare armour is allowed outside combat. Switching to or restocking backup
  armour during combat is not allowed.

## Banned Items And Mechanics

End Crystals, bed bombing, Respawn Anchor bombing, Enchanted Golden Apples,
Totems of Undying, tipped arrows, Thorns, Fire Aspect, Lunge, Punch, and
Netherite combat equipment are banned. The unique Netherite Chestplate is the
only Netherite armour exception.

## Cheats, Exploits, And Unfair Advantages

Hacking, X-ray, Freecam, unfair modified clients, duping, lag switching, lag
machines, glitching, unintended-mechanic exploits, pie-chart or F3+A abuse, and
other unfair advantages may result in an immediate ban. Replay mods are allowed
only for recording videos. Performance, accessibility, recording, and cosmetic
modifications are allowed only when they do not provide unavailable gameplay
information or abilities.

## Server Modifications And Events

- Invisible players' kills and deaths are hidden in chat during Week 1.
- Villagers automatically restock their trades.
- Breeze Rod drops are doubled.
- The Locator Bar will be enabled for 1 day. The date will be announced by the admins.
- The End opens 1 week after the server starts.
