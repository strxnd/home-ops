# GLITG SMP — Rules

## Minecraft Rules

### Ranks & Hearts (Ranked SMP)
- At the start of the SMP, all online players are assigned a random rank from #1 to #20.
- A player's rank sets their maximum health, potion duration, XP multiplier, and extra inventory slots.
- **Rank #1 has the maximum of 20 hearts.** Every rank below #1 has roughly half a heart less, down to Rank #20 at 10.5 hearts.
- **Rank stealing:** killing a player with a better rank swaps your rank with theirs. Climb to #1 through combat.
- Top 10 ranks gain extra inventory slots, accessible via `/extrainventory` (Rank #1–2 get a full double-chest-equivalent; Rank #10 gets 15 slots).
- There are no revives.

### Post-Death Rules
- There is no mechanical post-death PvP immunity.
- After dying, a player should normally not be intentionally killed again for 30 minutes.
- This 30-minute rule is **admin-enforced, not plugin-enforced**.
- A recently killed player may still be killed during this period if they are:
  - deliberately interfering with a fight;
  - stealing or picking up dropped loot;
  - blocking players from recovering loot;
  - provoking or repeatedly annoying another player;
  - attacking someone;
  - otherwise using their recent death to gain an unfair advantage.
- Being naked or unarmoured does not give a player absolute immunity from being killed.
- Repeatedly killing a recently killed player without a reasonable cause during their 30-minute protection period is not allowed.
- Killing a naked/unarmoured player without a valid reason is prohibited.
- Naked/unarmoured players may be killed if they are deliberately annoying, interfering, stealing loot, attacking, provoking, or otherwise causing a valid conflict.

Valid reasons include:
- stealing or taking your loot;
- picking up or interfering with dropped loot;
- attacking you;
- intentionally interfering with a fight;
- intentionally blocking you from recovering items;
- deliberately provoking or repeatedly annoying you.

### Teams
- A team may have a maximum of 2 high-tier players.
- High-tier refers to **skill level**; admins decide who counts as high-tier.
- One death does not automatically change someone's tier classification.

### Combat Rules
- While in combat, players may not:
  - use an Elytra;
  - use lava buckets;
  - use ice;
  - drain areas using buckets or sponges;
  - combat log;
  - danger log;
  - switch to or restock from backup armour sets.
- Players may still own, carry, store and prepare spare armour outside combat.
- The combat tag lasts **30 seconds**.

### Explosives
- Bed bombing is banned.
- Respawn Anchor bombing is banned.
- TNT minecarts may deal a maximum of **10 hearts** of damage to a player.

### Cheats, Exploits & Clients
The following may result in an immediate ban:
- Hacking
- X-ray
- Freecam
- Unfair modified clients
- Duping
- Lag switching
- Lag machines
- Glitch abuse
- Unintended exploit mechanics
- Pie chart/F3 abuse
- F3+A abuse
- Any other unfair gameplay advantage

Replay mods are allowed only for recording videos. Performance, accessibility, recording and cosmetic mods are allowed only if they do not provide gameplay information or abilities unavailable to normal players.

### Server Mechanics
- Villagers automatically restock their trades.
- Breeze Rod drops are doubled.
- Farms, redstone and automation must remain reasonably sized.
- Laggy farms or machines may be reduced or removed by admins.
- `/string` fills every empty inventory slot with String.

### Server Progression
- There is a **1-hour server-wide grace period** when the SMP begins. This is one global timer, not one hour for every player who joins.
- The Nether is open from the start.
- The End opens **1 week** after the SMP starts.
- The world border is **4,000 × 4,000**.

## Server Kit

### Armour
- Diamond armour is the maximum armour tier.
- Netherite armour is banned.
- Protection maximum: **Protection III**.
- Thorns is banned.

### Weapons
- Normal combat weapons may be Diamond maximum.
- Netherite swords are banned.
- Netherite combat axes are banned.
- Sharpness maximum: **Sharpness III**.
- Fire Aspect is banned.
- Lunge is banned.
- Riptide is banned.

### Maces
- **One mace may exist on the server at a time** (managed by OneMace).
- The mace may be carried and used, but **may not be stored in any container** (chests, ender chests, barrels, bundles, shulkers, etc.).
- Mace enchantment caps:
  - Density maximum: **III**
  - Breach maximum: **II**
  - Wind Burst maximum: **I**

### Netherite Tools
- Netherite is allowed only on:
  - Pickaxes
  - Shovels
  - Hoes

### Bows
- Power maximum: **Power IV**.
- Punch is banned.

### Ender Pearls
- Ender Pearls are not banned items.
- Players may obtain, carry, store, drop, pick up, and give them to other players.
- Players may **not** throw/use Ender Pearls. They cannot be used for teleportation.

### Potions
**Allowed**
- Strength II
- Speed I

Strength II lasts 8 minutes.

**Banned**
- Speed II
- Poison
- Turtle Master
- Slow Falling
- Weakness
- Slowness
- All tipped arrows

### PvP Inventory Limits
Maximum:
- Healing Potions (Instant Health II): **6 per type** (potion/splash/lingering)
- XP Bottles: **3 stacks / 192**
- Cobwebs: **2 stacks / 128**
- Golden Apples: **2 stacks / 128**
- Breeze Rods: **1 stack / 64**

### Banned Combat Items
Players may not carry/use:
- End Crystals
- Enchanted Golden Apples
- Totems
- Tipped Arrows
